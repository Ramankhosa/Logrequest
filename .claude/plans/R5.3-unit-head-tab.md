# R5.3 — "My Unit" Tab (Unit Head's Team View)

**Assigned to**: Opus (UI-heavy with person detail)
**Depends on**: R5.0 (scope resolver), R5.1 (shared components)
**Blocks**: R5.4 (reuses `PersonDetailSlideout`)

## ⚠ Finding 6 Fix: R5.3/R5.4 Dependency Resolved

R5.3 and R5.4 had a circular dependency: R5.3 referenced R5.4's drill-down API, and R5.4 reused R5.3's person-detail-slideout. Resolution:
- **R5.3 goes FIRST** (no backend dependency on R5.4)
- R5.3 uses its OWN unit-level API — `getOrgHierarchyStats` already exists in `dashboard-service.ts` (no need for R5.4's `getDrillDownNode`)
- R5.3 creates the `PersonDetailSlideout` component which R5.4 then imports
- R5.4 goes AFTER R5.3, adding the drill-down backend + advanced org browser

---

## Why This Exists

Unit heads (HODs, Deans, Team Leads) need a focused view of their direct team's KPI performance — who's on track, who's lagging, which KRAs are strong/weak. This is different from the Organization tab (which is hierarchy-wide drill-down). "My Unit" is about the people directly in your unit, with actionable detail per person.
